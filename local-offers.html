Home Ready for Baby – A Safe Home to Come Back To

HC
Hazelton City Council
Relationships & Support Networks Parenting relationships
Starts: 1 Oct 2026  ·  Ends: 1 Dec 2026
Who is it for:
Living in our area
Ages 16–25 Looked after Eligible Relevant Former relevant Qualifying

Bringing a baby home is a big moment, and it can bring worries about sleep, money, housing, heating, or not having everything ready. You don’t need a perfect home or expensive baby equipment... your baby just needs somewhere safe, warm, and suitable.

If you’re worried about not having a cot, furniture, heating, washing facilities, enough money, or the right housing, please tell us early. These things don’t mean you’ll be a bad parent; they’re practical problems we may be able to help with.

You can use the Home Essentials and Budgeting tools on Hazel to work out what you already have, what you still need, and what support you may be able to get. Your PA can help with benefits, grants, council support, repairs, housing issues, and planning for the baby’s arrival.

This support is for mums, dads, and non-birthing parents. You don’t have to do it alone, and you can choose who helps you.
