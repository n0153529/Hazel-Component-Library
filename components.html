Branding colours

Hazel's official brand colours, distinct from the functional UI tokens below. Use --brand-dark-green, --brand-light-green and --brand-dark-grey for marketing surfaces, logo lockups and anywhere the brand itself needs to show up, rather than the semantic --primary / --text-strong tokens used inside the product UI.
