Brand identity
Hazel brand colours

These are Hazel's official brand colours, distinct from the functional UI tokens above. Use --brand-dark-green, --brand-light-green and --brand-dark-grey for marketing surfaces, logo lockups and anywhere the brand itself needs to show up, rather than the semantic --primary / --text-strong tokens used inside the product UI.

Logo lockups

The cream logomark is designed to sit on the brand's dark surfaces, most often the dark green, sometimes the dark grey. Avoid placing it on light or white backgrounds.
